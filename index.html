Listen.

After you select text in any app, a small bubble appears right where your hand is already heading. Glide onto 🔊 and your Mac reads it in its Siri voice, at the speed you like.

  • 🔊
    No clicks, no shortcuts. Keep moving and rest on the speaker for a moment.
  • ✨
    Hear the short version. Rest on the sparkle for a quick AI summary, read aloud.
  • ⇕
    Scroll to select more. One notch grabs the next paragraph.
  • 〰️
    Shake to stop, circle to skip. A quick shake stops reading. Draw a circle to jump a sentence ahead or back.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Dictation uses a free Groq API key (the Setup Guide shows you how to get one in a minute), or runs fully offline on your Mac. Read Aloud never needs a key.

Why does macOS say it can't be opened?

SwipeSpeak isn't distributed through the App Store, so macOS asks before the first launch. Open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once; updates open normally.

What permissions does it need, and why?

Accessibility to notice when you select text and to type your dictation, Microphone for dictation, and macOS's own Speak Selection turned on for reading aloud. The Setup Guide takes you to each switch.

Where does my voice and text go?

There's no SwipeSpeak server. Dictation audio goes only to the transcription service you choose (Groq by default), or nowhere at all in offline mode. Reading aloud happens entirely on your Mac; text is only sent to Groq when you ask for a ✨ summary.

Which voice does it read with?

Your Mac's own Spoken Content voice. For the most natural sound, pick a Siri voice in System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content. The Setup Guide shows you where.

How do I get updates?

SwipeSpeak checks for new versions on its own and installs them with one click. You can also check any time from the menu bar.
