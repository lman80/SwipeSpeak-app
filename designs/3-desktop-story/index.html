FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon (M1 or later).

Why do I need “Open Anyway”?

SwipeSpeak isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks once. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Do I need an API key?

Only for fast cloud dictation and ✨ summaries: a free Groq key from console.groq.com/keys. Offline dictation and reading aloud need no key.

Where does my text go?

There's no SwipeSpeak server and no account. Reading aloud happens on your Mac. Text only leaves your Mac for ✨ summaries, and audio only for cloud dictation (both to Groq). Offline mode keeps dictation on the Mac.

Whose voice reads to me?

Your Mac's own Spoken Content voice (Siri voices), at the speed you set in System Settings.

What else can Dictation do?

Optional AI cleanup, spoken commands like “new line” and “bullet point”, Edit Mode for selected text, modes per app, words it learns, Paste Again, meeting recording, audio-file transcription and history.
