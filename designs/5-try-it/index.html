    QuestionsQuick answers

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.

    Which Macs?

    macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon (M1 or later).

    Why “Open Anyway”?

    SwipeSpeak isn't on the App Store, so the first time you open it macOS asks. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

    Do I need a key?

    Reading aloud and offline dictation need nothing. Fast cloud dictation and ✨ summaries use a free Groq key from console.groq.com/keys; the Setup Guide shows you how.

    What voice reads to me?

    Your Mac's own Spoken Content voice (the Siri voices), at the speed you set in System Settings.

    Does my text leave my Mac?

    Reading aloud happens on your Mac. Text only leaves for ✨ summaries, and audio only for cloud dictation, straight to Groq. There's no SwipeSpeak server. Offline mode keeps dictation on your Mac too.

    What else can Dictation do?

    Spoken formatting (“new line”, “bullet point”), Edit Mode (select text and say how to change it), modes per app, it learns your words, Paste Again, meeting recording, audio-file transcription and a history.
